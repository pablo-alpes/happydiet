The Problem

Nutrition is the only major health driver without objective, real-time measurement. We have wearables for heart rate, sleep, and steps — but diet tracking still relies on self-reported logs that most people abandon within weeks.

🏥

Clinical Blind Spot

Nutritionists prescribe plans, patients go home, and clinics have zero visibility until the next appointment — weeks later.

📝

Friction Kills Compliance

Manual food logging creates friction at the exact moment it matters: at the table, with food in front of you.

🔢

Calories ≠ Compliance

Existing tools count calories. But real compliance means following rules: forbidden items, allowed foods, daily limits — what doctors actually prescribe.

💔

Shame-Based Design

Diet culture punishes failure. Most apps tell you what you did wrong, not how to do better. HappyDiet coaches without judgment.

The Solution: The Intelligent Loop

HappyDiet automates nutrition tracking — from your meal to insight and compliance — using a 3-stage AI agent pipeline.

🔍

Precise + Fuzzy + Semantic

Three-layer matching: exact keywords, word overlap (≥ 0.6 similarity), and LLM semantic reasoning. "Bread" triggers "no gluten" — that's AI reasoning, not keyword matching.

📊

Rule Engine — Our Superpower

4 rule types: Forbidden, Allowed, Limit, Note. Transforms dietary logic into executable, persistent rules that enforce compliance meal after meal.

🔥

Calorie-Aware Scoring

Tracks daily kcal totals, applies progressive penalties for excess, and warns when you're approaching your target. A 4,000 kcal barbecue gets flagged — even if ingredients are compliant.

🎙️

Live Voice Coaching

Gemini Live API powers real-time voice conversations. The coach speaks naturally, supports barge-in, and responds to images mid-conversation.

Architecture

Entire stack runs on Google Cloud. The Gemini Live API is the conversational agent; the backend is the tool it calls. CI/CD: push to main → tests → prod deploy; push to dev → tests → dev deploy. Every layer is enclosed — nothing floats.

Social Impact

Making healthy eating accessible to everyone — not a tagline, but a design principle.

🌍 Accessibility

Voice-first, photo-based coaching removes barriers for the elderly, low-literacy populations, and people with disabilities. No typing, no searching, no calorie math.

🏥 Clinical Visibility

Nutritionists get objective adherence data between appointments — automatically, not self-reported. The dashboard is ready for the doctor's follow-up on day one.

💚 Coaching Without Shame

When you eat off-plan, the AI explains what happened and suggests what to do next. No guilt, no punishment. Lasting change starts with understanding.

🔬 Beyond Nutrition

The architecture scales: diabetes management, post-surgery diets, allergy tracking, medication compliance. The Rule Engine enforces whatever the clinician prescribes.

👓 Affordable Smart Lenses

HeyCyan integration — lenses under $100 for hands-free capture on iOS. The app works with the glasses; image transfer requires a manual trigger (iOS limits).
